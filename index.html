About This Tour

✦

Welcome to Southridge Farm. This interactive walking tour takes you through four of the most special and meaningful places on the property. These spaces highlight the value in balancing man-made architecture and creations alongside nature. Each stop provides unique utility within the natural environment: a quiet creative space, a gathering place, a contemplative orchard, and a space for physical activity.

Theme: Nature & Living Spaces — how contemporary humans extrapolate meaning from the natural environment.
